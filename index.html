Privacy Policy

The Deadville: Horde Survivors · CampStudio
Effective date: November 12, 2026
Android package: com.campstudio.thedeadville

This policy explains how CampStudio handles information when you play The Deadville: Horde Survivors (the “game”), contact us, or visit this policy page.

There is no CampStudio account or login. Progress and settings are saved on your device. The game uses Camp Analytics for usage information and Google AdMob for advertising. You can turn off Camp Analytics in Settings → Data → Share usage data. That switch does not control Google's advertising data.

1. Information handled by the game

  • Local game data: progress, unlocked content, virtual currency, rewards, settings, tutorial state and purchase-entitlement records are stored in the app's storage. CampStudio does not operate a cloud-save service for this game. Android backup or device-transfer features may preserve app data according to your device settings.
  • Usage and technical information: Camp Analytics receives a randomly generated app-installation identifier, session identifiers, platform, app version, operating-system version and device model. Events can include first launch, screens viewed, session duration, run results, selected characters and maps, levels, kills, virtual-currency activity, and advertising or purchase events when those features are used. The analytics client does not request your name, email address, advertising ID or precise location. Network services can receive an IP address as part of delivering a request; a random identifier does not make all of this information anonymous.
  • Advertising information: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device and app information, advertising interactions and diagnostics. The data processed depends on your device settings, region and the advertising features available in your version. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, detect fraud and maintain its services.
  • Payments: if in-app purchases are available in your version, Google Play handles payment and purchase records under its own policies. Game-related records may include the product, amount, currency and entitlement. CampStudio does not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. Please do not include passwords or payment-card details.

The game does not provide chat or ask for access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location. Its reminders are scheduled locally on your device, rather than through a CampStudio push-notification account.

2. Why information is used

Local data supports gameplay, settings, rewards and entitlements. Usage information helps us understand stability, performance, progression and game balance. Advertising services deliver and measure ads and help prevent abuse. Support information is used to answer your request. Payment providers also process information for payment security and legal obligations.

3. Services that receive information

  • Camp Analytics: CampStudio's analytics service receives the usage and technical information described above over HTTPS. It is separate from Google's advertising service.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play: these services process advertising and payment information under Google's Privacy Policy. See also Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.
  • GitHub Pages: this policy is hosted by GitHub. Visiting the page sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address, to GitHub. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page has no advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts added by CampStudio.

Information may be processed outside your country by these services. Their policies explain their processing, retention and international-transfer practices. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not provide other players with your local save or analytics identifier.

4. Your choices

  • Camp Analytics: open Settings → Data and turn off “Share usage data.” This stops future Camp Analytics event collection and clears the game's queued analytics events. It does not erase information already received by a server or change AdMob's settings.
  • Advertising: use Android's privacy and advertising controls to reset or delete your advertising ID and manage available advertising preferences. Google's My Ad Center provides additional controls where supported. Buying NO ADS removes interstitial ads between runs, not optional rewarded ads or all SDK processing.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the game's Settings → Data, or revoke the app's notification permission in Android Settings.
  • Local progress: the game's “Erase save” control removes game progress after confirmation. To remove all local app data, including settings and installation identifiers, use Android Settings → Apps → The Deadville → Storage → Clear storage/data. This can permanently erase your progress. Device backups may separately restore data.

5. Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Local game data remains until it is erased, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, subject to device backup and restore. Turning analytics off clears queued events on the device. Server-side usage and support information is retained as needed for the purposes described in this policy, security, resolving requests and applicable legal requirements; we do not promise that uninstalling the game deletes records already held by a service.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact CampStudio using the address below to make a request. Because there is no CampStudio account and analytics uses an installation identifier, we may need additional information to locate a relevant record. Requests about information held independently by Google or GitHub should also be directed to those providers. Erasing a save or uninstalling does not delete Google's purchase history, issue a refund or cancel a transaction.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal contact details. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us to request help and deletion where applicable. Advertising providers may still process technical information as described above; this is not a statement that no personal information is processed.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for the game's analytics transmissions and limit information to what is relevant to the features described here. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
